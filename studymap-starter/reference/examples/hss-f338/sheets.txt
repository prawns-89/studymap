=sheet Watch-outs | Where the notes contradict themselves. | cards
- Mullai :: The slide says union and happiness; tradition and the sample poem say waiting. Pick the slide's answer. | mullai
- Jacobi's five kandas :: Your notes say "Ayodhya to Uttara", which is six books; Ayodhya to Yuddha is five. The number to learn is five. | ramayana_tradition
- The Ramakatha in the Mahabharata :: The slide puts the most detailed telling in the Shanti Parva (Markandeya to Yudhishthira). In the epic itself, Markandeya's Rama story sits in the Aranyaka (Vana) Parva. For the quiz, follow the slide. | mahabharata
- Sangam dates :: The slides give three ranges (400 BCE–250 CE, 300 BCE–300 CE, 300 BCE–100 CE). Part 5 uses 300 BC–100 AD. | sangam
- Age of Indian literature :: "Almost 4,000 years" in Part 5; "the last 3,500 years" in Part 6. Match the wording of the question. | classical_canvas
- Tirukkural's date :: Part 5 puts the didactic phase in the 2nd–3rd c. AD; Part 6 dates the Tirukkural to about 500 CE or earlier. | tirukkural
- Prakrit's dates :: The Ashokan inscriptions (Prakrit) are the earliest dated records, yet Part 4 lists "the Prakrits (6th–11th centuries)" after Sanskrit. Don't let the second date trap you on Ashoka. | prakrit
- Itihaas :: Notes: "that which occurred". Past papers: "that which happened". Same answer. | indian_epics
- The lamp :: Notes: the Nilavilakku, a lamp with many wicks. Past papers: "base lamps". | nilavilakku
- Earliest Tamil work :: The Tolkappiyam is the earliest known or surviving work. Legend makes the lost Agattiyam older still. | tolkappiyam
- Bhakti in Tamil :: The key says "eating God", not "devotion". Devotion was the most common wrong answer. | bhakti
