%L | Light reactions | Stage 1 | In the thylakoid membranes
@light_reactions | Light-dependent reactions | Light reactions | 1
- Take place in the **thylakoid membranes** of the chloroplast.
- Use light energy to split water (**photolysis**), releasing **O₂** as a by-product. {exam}
- Produce **ATP** and **NADPH** for the Calvin cycle.
> calvin | supply ATP and NADPH
> chloroplast | happen in the thylakoids
@photosystems | Photosystems I and II | Photosystems | 2
- **PSII** (P680) acts first and splits water; **PSI** (P700) re-energises electrons to make NADPH. {exam}
- Named in order of discovery, not the order they work.
> light_reactions | the two engines
@etc | Electron transport chain | Electron transport | 2
- Carries electrons from PSII to PSI, pumping protons into the thylakoid space.
> atp_synthase | builds the proton gradient
@atp_synthase | ATP synthase | ATP synthase | 3
- Protons flowing back through it drive ATP formation (**chemiosmosis**).

%C | Calvin cycle | Stage 2 | In the stroma
@calvin | Calvin cycle (light-independent reactions) | Calvin cycle | 1
- Takes place in the **stroma**.
- Fixes **CO₂** into sugar using ATP and NADPH. {exam}
- Three phases: **fixation, reduction, regeneration** of RuBP.
> rubisco | its first enzyme
@rubisco | RuBisCO | RuBisCO | 1
- Enzyme that fixes CO₂ onto **RuBP**; probably the most abundant protein on Earth. {extra}
- Also reacts with O₂ (**photorespiration**), which wastes energy.
> c4_cam | the problem C4 and CAM solve
@g3p | G3P, the sugar output | G3P | 2
- Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate: the three-carbon sugar that leaves the cycle.
- Two G3P make one **glucose**.
> calvin | the product

%S | Structure | Where | The chloroplast
@chloroplast | Chloroplast | Chloroplast | 1
- Double-membrane organelle with **thylakoids** stacked into **grana**, surrounded by **stroma**.
- Contains its own DNA. {extra}
@pigments | Pigments | Pigments | 2
- **Chlorophyll a** is the main pigment; chlorophyll b and **carotenoids** are accessory pigments.
- Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light and reflects **green**. {exam}
> photosystems | sit in the photosystems

%F | Limits | Factors | What speeds it up or slows it down
@limiting | Limiting factors | Limiting factors | 1
- **Light intensity**, **CO₂ concentration** and **temperature**. {exam}
- The factor in shortest supply sets the rate.
> rubisco | temperature affects the enzyme
@c4_cam | C4 and CAM plants | C4 & CAM | 2
- **C4** (maize, sugarcane) separate CO₂ capture in space; **CAM** (cacti, pineapple) separate it in time, opening stomata at night.
> limiting | adaptations to heat and drought

%O | Overview | Big picture | The whole process
@photosynthesis | Photosynthesis | Photosynthesis | 1
- 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. {exam}
- Converts light energy into chemical energy stored in sugar.
> light_reactions | stage 1
> calvin | stage 2
@respiration | Link to respiration | Respiration | 3
- Cellular respiration runs roughly the reverse equation, releasing the stored energy.
> photosynthesis | the mirror image
